I started in 2022 at Versatec, in Caratinga, delivering websites and integrations for local clients —
on site, in a team, with the client on the other side of the desk. From there the work kept getting
deeper: as a freelancer I began building software from scratch, at Agio I joined a team with code
review and refinement, and today I keep an education ecosystem running in production, from the
database schema to the student screen.

I like work that can be checked afterwards: an API contract the CI verifies, tests that run on every
Pull Request, versioned migrations. When the decision is written down and the gate runs in CI,
whoever touches the code next does not have to ask me what I meant.

I have worked with a lot of autonomy: decisions and execution went through me, and that teaches you
fast. It also has a cost — with little peer review, the mistake I do not catch takes longer to
surface. That is why I am looking for an engineering team with collaboration, code review and
technically strong people to grow with.
